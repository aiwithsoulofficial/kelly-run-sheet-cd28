How to use this page

Keep this open on your phone or iPad at the front of the room. On Monday, the block you're in gets a rust outline and a "Right now" bar appears at the top with the minutes left.

  • Grey text boxes are exact words people type. Tap Copy to copy one, then paste it into the group chat or on screen.
  • Everything inside "quote marks in italics" is a line you can say out loud, word for word, if you want to.
  • Every block tells you what it's for, what they do, how you'll know it worked, and what to do if it doesn't.

Word list

The technical words used on this page, in plain English.

Terminal
The Mac app where people type commands. Open it with Cmd + Space, type "Terminal", press Enter. On Windows the same thing is called PowerShell.
Claude Code
Claude running on their own laptop. They start it by typing claude in Terminal. It can open, write and save files, which the chat website can't.
My-Business folder
The folder the install made on their laptop. Everything about their business lives here. To work in it, they type cd ~/My-Business then claude.
Skill
A job Claude knows how to do well, like editing a video or writing a follow-up email. They start one by typing a slash and its name, like /induction, or by asking in plain English.
aws-update
A command that downloads the newest version of all their skills. Since they installed, three new skills have been added: a live dashboard, rostering and reconciliation. This command gets them.
Foundational Folders Kit
A set of files inside My-Business that describe their business: what they sell and for how much, who buys, how they talk, their reviews, their policies, their brand. Every skill reads these files before it writes anything, so it sounds like them.
/induction
The skill that fills in the Foundational Folders Kit by interviewing them. They answer out loud or typed.
Key (or API key)
A long password a tool like Stripe or GoHighLevel gives out, so another program can read your data. Each tool has a page in its settings to make one.
.env file
A private file inside My-Business where keys are pasted. Keys go there, never into the chat with Claude.
Dashboard
A web page that runs on their own laptop and shows their money, bookings, leads and customers from all their connected tools, in one place. They open it by typing my-dashboard in Terminal.
CSV export
A spreadsheet download most tools offer (usually under Reports or Export). If a tool can't be connected with a key today, they download a CSV and drag it into the My-Business/data folder instead.
Telegram and BotFather
Telegram is a free messaging app. BotFather is the official Telegram account that makes a private "bot" (their own chat contact). Once it's set up, they message their bot from their phone and Claude does the job on their laptop.

Block 2: Drop their own content into the folders

Their real material is in the system: logo, photos, price list, old emails, reviews, videos.

Where things go

  • Price lists, policies, old newsletters, website text, reviews: straight into Foundational-Folders-Kit.

Then Claude sorts it, in Claude

Read everything I just dropped into my Foundational Folders Kit and update the right files: offers, proof (real reviews word for word), FAQs, voice and brand. Show me what you changed.
Set my brand colours and fonts for my videos from my logo and brand files.
  • Worked when: Claude lists what it updated, and the prices and reviews match their real ones.

Block 3: Connect their tools to Claude

The magic, part 3: run it from their phone

They send a voice message from their phone and get the answer back from Claude, and a morning update is set for 7am tomorrow.

Make their bot (on their phone)

  • Open Telegram, search for @BotFather (the one with the blue tick), send the message /newbot.
  • Give it any name, then a username ending in "bot", like SarahsBusinessbot.
  • BotFather replies with a long key. Copy it.

Then, in Terminal (Mac)

bash ~/My-Business/.system/telegram/setup-telegram.sh
  • Paste the key when it asks, then follow the steps it shows.

The moment

  • They send their bot a voice note: "What came in today, and what's on tomorrow?"
  • Worked when: the bot replies with the answer.
  • Then set tomorrow's 7am update, in Terminal:

  • Tell them plainly: the laptop has to be on and awake for this to work.
The bot doesn't reply: the setup line wasn't run, or the laptop went to sleep. Run the setup line again with the lid open and plugged in. Windows: this part is Mac-only for now. Pair them with a Mac owner to watch.
